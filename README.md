# 🛒 SuperMarket 360 — Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

SuperMarket 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze supermarket sales performance, product category trends, and inventory levels. It helps business users monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), understand sales patterns, and identify products that require replenishment.

**Note:** This portfolio project uses synthetic/sample data for demonstration purposes.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total sales, gross profit, and average bill value.
- Analyze monthly sales trends and store-level performance.
- Compare category sales, quantities sold, and profit margins.
- Identify top-performing products and customer payment preferences.
- Monitor inventory value, stock quantities, and replenishment requirements.
- Highlight low-stock and out-of-stock items for timely action.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
- Key performance indicators for sales and profitability.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Sales performance by store.
- Gross profit by product category.

### 2. Category Analysis
- Sales and quantities sold by category.
- Profit margin comparisons.
- Top 10 products by sales revenue.
- Sales breakdown by payment method.

### 3. Inventory Analysis
- Total inventory value and stock units.
- Products requiring replenishment.
- Inventory status analysis.
- Reorder alerts with conditional formatting.

## 🧮 Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Gross Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Bills
- Average Bill Value
- Total Stock Units
- Inventory Value
- Products to Reorder

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- Excel / Synthetic Sample Dataset

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard demonstrates how sales and inventory data can be transformed into actionable insights. It helps users identify sales trends, compare category profitability, recognize top-performing products, and prioritize inventory replenishment.
