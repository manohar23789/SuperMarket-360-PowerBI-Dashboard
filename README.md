# 🛒 SuperMarket 360 — Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
SuperMarket 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze supermarket sales, profitability, customer activity, and inventory performance across multiple stores.

This portfolio project demonstrates how business data can be transformed into interactive visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor total sales and gross profit.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across stores.
- Identify product categories contributing the most gross profit.
- Track customer activity and average bill value.
- Support business performance monitoring through interactive filters.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Executive Overview
- **Total Sales**
- **Gross Profit**
- **Total Bills**
- **Profit Margin %**
- **Total Customers**
- **Total Quantity Sold**
- **Average Bill Value**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Store
- Gross Profit by Product Category

### Interactive Filters
- Store Name
- Category
- Date

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX Measures
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- Business Intelligence and Performance Analysis

## 💡 Business Questions Answered
1. What are the overall sales and gross profit?
2. How does sales performance change month by month?
3. Which stores generate the highest sales?
4. Which product categories contribute the most gross profit?
5. How do results change when different stores, categories, or dates are selected?
